## Root cause

The delta UPSERT assigned EXCLUDED.response_text unconditionally. The reviews_delta fallback omitted max_reviews and used apply_complete_review_snapshot.

## Approved fix

Preserve stored reply fields when an incoming delta has no reply; cap reviews_delta Apify fallback at 50 and persist it through the delta UPSERT. Explicit reviews_full behavior remains unchanged.

**Why this is causal:** The changed UPSERT controls the destructive overwrite directly, and the worker branch now supplies the paid Actor limit and avoids complete-snapshot replacement for scheduled deltas.
